# Evaluation

Generated by `python evaluate.py`. Counts below are from that run.

## Setup

`claims_history.csv` has 3862 rows and 3830 distinct claim ids. 32 extra rows are exact copies and are dropped so a paid claim is not drawn down twice. `claims_to_check.csv` has 546 claims. Authorizations: 780. EVV visits: 5076.

The engine never reads the claim it is deciding. Three ledgers are scored:

- **Known priors.** Earlier history claims draw units from the outcome on the file. This is the ledger a biller has once those claims are adjudicated.
- **Open loop.** Every history claim draws units only if the engine itself would pay it. A wrong pay or deny changes later balances.
- **Holdout.** Claims submitted before 2026-10-01 use the recorded outcome. Claims submitted on or after that date use the engine, and only those later claims are scored. The cut sits after both bulletin dates (1 August filing, 1 September weekend modifier), so the holdout still contains both versions of each rule.

A fourth pass puts `claims_to_check.csv` on the same submission queue. That is the ledger `predict.py` uses. It is reported as a finding, not as the headline, because those claims have no outcome of their own.

## Findings left out of the rules

- D99 accounts for 73 of the known-prior misses. The manuals define the code and do not say when it is used. Encoding it would mean memorising claim ids.
- D10 accounts for 35 misses. Section 9.5 of the Summit handbook says additional contract terms may apply, and the remarks name amendment 3, but history pays partial-hour claims on authorizations that also produce D10. The correction claims in the unchecked file do round those units down to a multiple of 4, which is consistent with the remark and still does not say which partial hours are denied.
- 8 member-days have more than one verified visit. Summing those minutes versus keeping the longest visit would change the D09 decision on 0 history claims. The engine sums them because the help article says same-day visits are added and the claim does not name a caregiver.
- History units are already 15-minute units (largest billed value 40). No row stored minutes in the units column.
